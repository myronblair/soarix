If you are visiting this site

The website you just visited is having problems, or is being set up. Please try again later.

To let the administrators know you saw this page and not what you expected, an e-mail to webmaster at the site's domain should reach the right person. For example, if you saw this page while visiting www.example.com, you could write to webmaster@example.com.

If you are the website administrator

Add your website's files and this page goes away. Until you do, visitors see this page and not your content.

  • Apache: put your files in /var/www/html/. To stop this page from ever showing, follow the notes in /etc/httpd/conf.d/welcome.conf.
  • nginx: put your files in a location of your choice and set the root line in /etc/nginx/nginx.conf. (This page is served from /usr/share/nginx/html/.)

Turn services on and off with soarix-server, or the Server Services window.
